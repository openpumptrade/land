    Sheet 4

    General notes

    1. 1Claim

      The engine wallet claims the coin's creator rewards from pump.fun. SOL, on chain, with the signature as receipt.

    2. 2Convert

      Everything above a gas reserve is swapped on Solana through Jupiter into USDC, the only collateral the Parcl bridge accepts.

    3. 3Bridge

      The USDC is deposited into the Parcl bridge program on Solana (minimum $5 per deposit) and credited to the Parcl account owned by the very same key. No third party in between.

    4. 4Buy square feet

      Free collateral becomes long positions on the city indices in the target book, furthest-under-weight first, with IOC market orders at 1x: notional equals collateral, nothing borrowed. One unit of a market is one square foot of its index.

    5. 5Hold

      Parcl Labs publishes each index once a day (7:50 AM ET). Positions are marked to that print; the only carry is the hourly funding rate. The engine never sells.

    6. 6Repeat

      Every 10 minutes.

    7. 7Ownership

      Square feet here are index exposure on Parcl, not deeds. Nothing is custodied by this site: the Parcl account belongs to the engine wallet's key and every position is public on the validator.
