The fine print, on chain

Rules nobody can bend.

01Rewards only

The engine never touches the supply. It only claims creator rewards, the same ones any pump.fun creator receives.

02One key, one account

The Parcl account is owned by the engine wallet's key. Every position is public on the validator; nothing is held by this site.

03Equal weight, eight cities

USA, NYC, Chicago, LA, Miami, Austin, DC and San Francisco, 12.5% each. Orders go to the city furthest under its weight.

041x, no borrowing

Notional equals collateral. A daily-marked index at 1x can lose value, but it cannot be liquidated by a wick.

05Never sold

There is no sell instruction in the engine. Square feet accumulate for as long as the coin trades.

06A receipt at every hop

Claim, swap, bridge, order: each one is a transaction you can open yourself. See the receipts →

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is LAND?

A pump.fun coin whose creator rewards are converted into exposure to U.S. housing prices on Parcl, measured in square feet of city indices, instead of going to a creator wallet.

Is it real property?

No. Square feet here are index exposure on Parcl V4, perpetual markets quoted in USD per square foot of a Parcl Labs city index. No deed, no building, no tenant.

Is it a tax coin?

No. $LAND is a regular pump.fun coin with the standard SOL pair, no tax and no custom mechanics on the token itself. Only the creator rewards are used.

Who holds the position?

The Parcl account owned by the engine wallet's key. It is public on the Parcl validator and linked from the portfolio page, alongside every transaction.

Can the position be sold?

The engine has no sell path. It claims, converts, bridges and buys. The only carry is the hourly funding rate of each market.

How often does it run?

Every 10 minutes. A deposit needs at least 5 USDC and an order at least 5 USD, so small cycles accumulate until there is enough to move.

01

Claim

The engine wallet claims the coin's creator rewards from pump.fun. SOL, on chain, with the signature as receipt.

02

Convert

Everything above a gas reserve is swapped on Solana through Jupiter into USDC, the only collateral the Parcl bridge accepts.

03

Bridge

The USDC is deposited into the Parcl bridge program on Solana (minimum $5 per deposit) and credited to the Parcl account owned by the very same key. No third party in between.

04

Buy square feet

Free collateral becomes long positions on the city indices in the target book, furthest-under-weight first, with IOC market orders at 1x: notional equals collateral, nothing borrowed. One unit of a market is one square foot of its index.

05

Hold

Parcl Labs publishes each index once a day (7:50 AM ET). Positions are marked to that print; the only carry is the hourly funding rate. The engine never sells.
